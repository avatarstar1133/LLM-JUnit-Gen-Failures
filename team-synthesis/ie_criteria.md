# Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria
- IC1: Study concerns automated or LLM-assisted software test generation.
- IC2: Study evaluates generated tests/code using compilability, executability, correctness, coverage or a closely related quality metric.
- IC3: Study reports enough method detail to identify dataset/model/evaluation.
- IC4: Full text is accessible.
- IC5: English-language research publication or credible preprint relevant to the RQ.

## Exclusion Criteria
- EC1: Not related to software testing or generated tests.
- EC2: Opinion/blog/marketing content without empirical evidence.
- EC3: Duplicate report of the same experiment; keep the most complete version.
- EC4: Full text unavailable.
- EC5: Does not report evidence useful for this RQ.
