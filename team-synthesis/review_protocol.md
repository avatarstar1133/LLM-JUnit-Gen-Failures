# Literature Review Protocol

## Objective
Collect empirical evidence related to LLM-generated unit/JUnit tests, compilability, executability, compiler-feedback repair, structured prompting and task-dependent failure.

## Shared Search String
```text
("unit test generation" OR "JUnit test suite" OR HumanEval)
AND
(ChatGPT OR Gemini OR "compiler-feedback repair" OR "structured prompting")
AND
(compilability OR executability OR "build success rate" OR "test execution rate")
```

## Workflow
1. Run the same shared query.
2. Record query date and raw hits.
3. Export/store citations or metadata.
4. Deduplicate.
5. Title/abstract screening.
6. Full-text screening.
7. Record exactly one primary exclusion reason for excluded full texts.
8. Fill the shared evidence table.
